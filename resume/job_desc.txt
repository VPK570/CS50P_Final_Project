Job Description (Frontend Developer – React)

Position: Frontend Developer (React.js)
Location: Remote / Tampa, FL
Experience: 3–6 years

Job Summary:
We are seeking a talented Frontend Developer with strong experience in React.js to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have a proven track record of delivering high-quality web applications, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and optimizing performance.

Responsibilities:

Develop and maintain modern web applications using React.js and Node.js.

Implement state management using Redux or Flux patterns.

Optimize application performance and ensure responsive design.

Collaborate with UX/UI designers and backend developers to integrate APIs.

Follow best practices for code quality, testing, and documentation.

Participate in CI/CD pipeline and DevOps processes.

Requirements:

3+ years of professional experience with React.js and JavaScript.

Strong knowledge of HTML, CSS, and modern frontend frameworks.

Experience with Redux, Flux, and component lifecycle optimization.

Familiarity with Node.js and backend integration.

Understanding of CI/CD and DevOps practices.

Excellent communication skills and team collaboration.

Experience with data visualization and performance optimization is a plus.

Preferred Qualifications:

Knowledge of PHP, SQL (MySQL/MongoDB), and C++ is a bonus.

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field.

Prior experience in delivering enterprise-level web applications.
